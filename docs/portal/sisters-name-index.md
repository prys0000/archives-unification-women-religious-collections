# Sisters Name Index

## Purpose

The Sisters Name Index provides cross-congregational, person-centered discovery that cannot be achieved through collection title searching alone.

## Research Problem

Women religious may be documented under:

- civil names
- baptismal names
- religious names
- initials
- surname variants
- titles
- translated forms
- different dates or incomplete dates

Repository-level discovery may therefore separate records that relate to the same person.

## Functional Layers

### Identity Record

Stores structured biographical and naming information.

### Variant Management

Retains alternate forms rather than discarding them after reconciliation.

### Faceted Discovery

Supports searching/filtering across biographical dimensions.

### Archival Relationship

Connects a person to related collections or descriptive records.

## Recommended Facets

- congregation
- record status
- birth decade
- birth country/state/region
- vocation/work
- mission country/state/region
- entrance decade
- first profession decade
- final profession decade
- death decade
- departure status
- language

## Interface Principle

Expandable in-place records reduce unnecessary navigation and support rapid comparative research.

## Authority Principle

The index should separate:

- preferred/display form
- variant forms
- evidentiary dates
- congregation
- archival source
- archivist notes

## Deduplication Principle

Do not merge solely because two records share a surname or religious name.

Use combinations of stable attributes and preserve ambiguity when evidence is insufficient.

## Future Research Value

A reconciled person layer can support:

- cross-repository biographical research
- ministry network analysis
- geographic movement
- congregation comparisons
- temporal research
- improved chatbot entity recognition
